        Engine and games

        The blend works like this: with n games of your own, your expected score is (n × yours + this number × peers) ÷ (n + this number). Sample sizes are shown next to every statistic.

        Hidden gems and better moves

        A hidden gem must pass all three. A "better" move must have the minimum games and score that many points more than your move. Small samples are noisy, so keep the game count high.

        Repertoire backup

        Export writes the colour selected at the top as PGN with variations. Import adds every move in a pasted PGN to that colour, variations included. Nothing is removed: a different move of yours where you already have one becomes another option.
